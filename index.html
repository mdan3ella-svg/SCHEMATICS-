Workflow · Discipline / Layer

Color and lineweight are applied to 3D drafting overlays and regenerated vector sheets.

Choose a layer, draw an element, then select the object to modify it. Hidden or locked layers cannot be edited.

Precision drafting / Snaps

MSAA is requested at renderer startup. Supersampling and soft shadows improve edge appearance, subject to GPU limits.

Drafting snaps apply to new points and dragged vertices. Shift forces orthogonal tracking. Vertex handles are shown for the selected unlocked object.

Precision geometry · Civil documentation

Arc by 3 picked points · persistent dimensional and orientation constraints · derived dimension labels · CAD plot at declared physical paper dimensions. Geometry and numeric checks are preliminary, not survey or engineering certification.

04 — Toposurface / Connections

Selected road or bridge inherits current terrain elevation. Adjust vertical offset and longitudinal grade; regenerate when topography changes. Join road-to-road, road-to-bridge or road-to-intersection by nearest endpoints.

Circular centerline fillets, graded bridge approaches and live corridor terrain cuts are generated from object parameters. These are triangulated surface models, not exact B-rep solids; validate all crossings and design standards.
